Y Combinator decline is here

has someone experienced agent rejection? when a new startup or pitch has the word "agent" in it, it just completely makes me roll my eyes and just not pay attention, is like my brain switches off. You start at -10 points, you better give me somthing goooood.

i fear that i cannot be the only one.

Y combinator is starting to feel the place where no human lives, just ai. i feel they need a rebranding. is it me or AI is just a little out of fashion? I feel like the key now is not use AI for everything, or at least not say you use ai for everything, please give me a person, a software that works, a human detail?

I miss the days when it was just the software and me, facing each other, no more outsiders. I love my plain textedit, i know what to do and what to expect. no noise, no comments, no recommendations. i like my own taste, thanks for completely make me question myself!!

i used to envy these Y combinator guys, they looked cool, having fun, creating the new tech companies. now, i look and them and is like i feel bad for them? mbye is just me i am feed and bombarded with ai. ai ai ai ai.

tbh can we start not using the word ai for basically every development in the world and use more technical words, like i am developing a recommender system with ai. this gives me 0 information, like are just using an llm to decide?, are you training a machine learning model? what are you doing!!! did the president himself told you the architecture of your development is a state secret and the country is hanging by a thread bc you will disclouse your mid architecture development????
