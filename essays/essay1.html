Y Combinator decline is here

Has someone else experienced "agent" rejection? The moment a new startup or pitch has the word "agent" in it, my brain just switches off — you start at -10 points, so you'd better give me something good.

I fear I'm not the only one.

Y Combinator is starting to feel like a place where no humans live, just AI. I think they need a rebrand. Is it just me, or is AI a little out of fashion right now? I feel like the key now is to not use AI for everything — or at least not say you use AI for everything. Please, give me a person, a piece of software that works, a human detail.

I miss the days when it was just the software and me, facing each other, no outsiders. I love my plain text editor — I know what to do and what to expect. No noise, no comments, no recommendations. I like my own taste — thanks for making me completely question myself.

I used to envy the Y Combinator crowd — they looked cool, having fun, building new tech companies. Now I look at them and almost feel bad for them? Maybe it's just me, fed up and bombarded with AI. AI, AI, AI, AI.

Honestly, can we stop using the word "AI" for basically every development in the world and use more technical words instead? Saying "I'm developing a recommender system with AI" gives me zero information. Are you just using an LLM to decide something? Are you training a machine learning model? What are you actually doing? Did the president himself tell you your architecture is a state secret, and the country is hanging by a thread if you disclose your model's design?
