I wanted to build ai... now i want to destroy it

back in 2023 i was so excited to learn about llms. it was an exciting time for tech lovers. the possibilities were endless, and the room for improvement was so so wide. the excitement was boiling. It remembered me when i started to do pilates reformer. i was so bad at it, but what comforted me was thinking i would have the most improvement in the room.

now, is not that i dont want to know more, is not i dont want to become better. Is that i want to destroy it. Is the famous video “when your friend from a tech leaves the job - i had fun, onto a new adventure!!, and when your friend from a big tech leaves the job - i am going to join a yoga retreat in bali and retire”. Now, the next level is going to be i am creating a company to destroy AI.

Is my job still in AI? Yes. So am I a hypocrite? totally yes! I thought about million things to make society redirect. Building tools to block certain AI content, plug ins to warn people (like tobacco advertise), building a complete different AI interaction for ppl. Sometimes I wonder if I have just simply lost faith in humanity.

responsible ai is my only chance to believe in AI godness. My faith is hanging by a thread. This thread is called ai governance. Big techs have to, need to, must, should, absolutely respect user privacy and be transparent and respectful with their data. Not (only) for society well being. but because if they dont do it, this wave anti-ai will eat them up alive. So they better make inside changes.

This is why I am going to get deep with Responsible ai. I will un-mask every single privacy hole and have no mercy. I decided to have a leap of faith, and take justice with my own hands. I will try to uncover privacy “legal loopholes” from big techs. Why? I have a superpower. I understand all the underlying math / computer science work related with llms. I need to use it for people that are completly ignorants, - like my parents , no disrespect - and make them save.
