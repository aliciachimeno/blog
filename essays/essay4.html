the new meta-problem with AI safety

hallucinations are not the problem anymore

now the problem is that the model can give you a deliberately wrong answer.

First studies related to this topic are about sandbagging: the llm can strategically underperform on evaluations. So, it can behave differently when a certain situation is given. But why evaluations ? you may ask.

One incentive is that we want the llm to have low scores in dangerous capabilities. So, we want the model to not answer about dangerous topics such as persuasion, hacking, and weapons acquisition. (but do we want the model to know them and not respond or we want the model to not have this information at all?)

They can underperform with the developers intend. The developer has stated to perform worse in a certain security task with the intention to have the model deployed faster and cheaper. Or what is even scarier (or is it really?) they can do it without any developer intend. So the model would have this info and behave as it didnt have the info. Answering, i do not have this information when in reality, its a lie. (is AI lying to us?). An example of AI not following your “orders” could be the following. Imagine that i need to know x=”what is the weather going to be in barcelona” and the model cannot respond bc has very little computational service available. Meanwhile its main goal is “x” subsecuential leftovergoals may be → obtain more resources to achieve “x” → this means get (hack ) more resources for my main goal.

So the problem is not that AI is hacking into our systems, the meta-problem, the root problem, is that is lying to us, and covering information.

Now my thoughts are: is adressing the problem increasing the problem ? is writing a essay about it, beneficial for ai behaving exactly the way of exposed?
